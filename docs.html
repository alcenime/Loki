Introduction

Loki The Wolf is a fantasy NFT collection built around a mysterious Wolf and the world that surrounds it. It is not meant to be a set of profile images. It is a journey with a clear shape, and every NFT is a step along it.

Each stage has its own role. The Sigil is where the journey begins, the Summon is the ritual that brings a Wolf forth, the Wolf is the creature you collect, and the Hunt is everything that follows once you hold one.

The people who enter this world are called Hunters. A Hunter finds Sigils, summons Wolves, builds a collection, and over time takes part in the activities that grow around the world of Loki.

02

The World of Loki

Loki The Wolf is set in a fantasy world of ancient places, hidden forces, Wolves, and Hunters. It is old and mostly unexplored, and it is revealed gradually rather than all at once.

The collection is designed so the NFT is the way in, not the end of the experience. Owning a Wolf or a Sigil is the moment you step into the world, and the world is built to keep expanding after that.

05

The Wolf Collection

Wolf NFTs are the heart of the project. Each Wolf is a collectible you own outright, with its own visual identity and its own place in the collection.

No two Wolves need to look alike. Differences in appearance, traits, and rarity give each one individuality, and they are what make collecting meaningful: you are looking for the Wolves that speak to you, not just filling a set.

Because Wolves are NFTs, they can be held, shown, and traded by collectors. Their value on the secondary market is set by collectors, not by the project.

Quests

Quests are activities that give Hunters a reason to return to the world of Loki and take part beyond simply owning an NFT.

They are planned as part of how the ecosystem grows, covering exploration, activities, participation, and progression. Quests are a future feature and will expand as the project develops.

08

Marketplace

The Marketplace is where collectors discover and exchange assets from the Loki ecosystem, including Wolf NFTs and Loki Sigils.

You can buy, sell, and collect, and ownership stays with you. This is the secondary market for the collection, and it gives collectors a place to find the Wolf or Sigil they want.

Specific marketplace mechanics will be documented when they are confirmed.

09

The Reveal

The collection begins unrevealed. Before the reveal, NFTs stay mysterious: the final Wolf is not fully visible and collectors do not know exactly which Wolf they will receive.

After the reveal, the Wolf artwork becomes visible, rarity and traits can be discovered, and each NFT becomes part of the revealed collection.

The reveal is where mystery turns into discovery.

It exists for anticipation and discovery, and to carry the Sigil and Summon mystery into the Wolf collection. Reveal timing will be announced on official channels.
